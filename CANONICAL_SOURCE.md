# CNAUS Canonical Source (Non-Core)

## 1. Canonical Source of the CNAUS Standard

The canonical source of the CNAUS Standard is the CNAUS Root Authority repository
and its published immutable tags (releases).

For the avoidance of doubt:
- The `main` branch is the canonical working branch.
- Git tags `vX.Y.Z` are immutable snapshots and MUST be treated as the authoritative
  reference for a specific released version.

## 2. Forks and Mirrors

Forks and third-party mirrors may exist for review or convenience. However, a fork
or mirror is not canonical unless explicitly designated by the CNAUS Root Authority.

## 3. Normative vs Non-Core Documents

The CNAUS Core Standard is defined by its normative specifications as identified
by the published releases. Non-Core documents (informative guidance, reference
tools, examples, conformance vectors, and schemas) may be provided to support
implementation and verification but do not expand the normative scope unless a
release explicitly designates them as normative.
